Important: The tool first requests historical tick data, then subscribes to live ticks. Before each live tick, it makes a simple sample-based digit suggestion from the preceding window and checks that suggestion against the actual next digit. The percentage is the observed hit rate in this session, not a guaranteed future probability. Digit-frequency analysis cannot reliably predict random future ticks. No trades are placed.
